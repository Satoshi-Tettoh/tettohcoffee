TETTOH COFFEE Website
=====================

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- assets/

How to use:
1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Replace placeholder areas with actual images.
3. Replace info@example.com with the real contact email.
4. Adjust texts as needed.
5. Upload the files to your hosting service.

Recommended image sizes:
- Hero: 2400px wide or more, landscape
- Works: 1800px wide or more
- Objects: 1600px square or more

Important:
This version intentionally avoids a typical coffee-shop layout.
The design treats TETTOH COFFEE as an archive of works, objects and records.
